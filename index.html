01 THE LIP

Frost glass, cut at the mouth so the liquid must hesitate.

The flacon is frosted glass under a pink gold mat collar. The lip is polished to a radius that holds a bead for roughly two seconds before gravity wins — long enough for the perfume to warm to skin temperature at the edge. Structure and flow, held in one gesture.

02 RELEASE

It elongates before it falls. That neck is the dose.
