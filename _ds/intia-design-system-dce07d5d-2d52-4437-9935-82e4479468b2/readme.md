# INTIA — Design System

**INTIA** (formerly **BACALER**) is a prestige hair-fragrance and hair-longevity house. Category line: **SCENTual hair care**. Tagline: **Silent Radiance. Soft Strength. Still Indulgence.** Core pillar: **"Where radiance whispers."**

> INTIA is beauty in quiet form. It's the stillness between breath and glow — where radiance feels calm, and sensuality feels grounded.

Hero products: **The Perfume** (hair perfume, 50 ml) and **The Lotion / Cream**. Manufacturer and responsible person on pack: *Biopharma Laboratories Italy s.r.l., Via delle Rose no. 48, 40136 Bologna, Italy*. PAO 12M.

## Positioning
- **Silent luxury**: presence without performance, confidence without excess. Science meets softness; structure meets soul; care becomes ritual.
- **Not about more — about enough.** A return to presence, balance, quiet confidence.
- Against the market: bond-builders (K18, Olaplex) have science without sensory ritual; niche hair perfumes (Byredo, Diptyque) have scent but dry the hair with alcohol. INTIA heals while it scents.
- Technology named in the materials: **KeraBio K31** (~900 Da biomimetic keratin, cortex-level repair) and **Polyfix ZRC** odour capture (zinc ricinoleate binds odour molecules rather than masking them); **KeraDuo** for the overnight scalp+hair serum.
- Target: women 25–55, independent, emotionally fluent, drawn to tactile experience, design and conscious living. *"Women choose INTIA not to perform femininity, but to return to it."*

## The philosophy — dualities
Earth × Ether · Structure × Flow · Precision × Emotion · Industrial × Intimate.
Design language: **Industrial Minimalism × Emotional Texture**.

# CONTENT FUNDAMENTALS

**Voice pillars (verbatim from the brief):** *Calm Power* — confident, but never loud. *Refined Sensuality* — feminine, never fragile. *Modern Poise* — smart, elegant, intentional. *Textural Emotion* — words that feel tactile, visual and grounding.

- **Person.** The house speaks as **we**; the reader is **you**, and only for instruction ("Spray onto dry hair from 15–20 cm"). Never "I".
- **Three registers, deliberately layered.** *Sensory* ("the cool touch of metal on skin, the light shimmering through pearl"), *philosophical* ("It's not about more — it's about enough"), *clinical* ("~900 Da", "zinc ricinoleate"). A page usually holds two of the three; the mix is the brand.
- **Sentence shape.** Short declaratives and fragments used as beats. Triads are the signature rhythm: *"Silent Radiance. Soft Strength. Still Indulgence."* · *"Formulas that work. Textures that ground. Design that breathes."* · *"Repair, refresh, recharge."* Semicolons used properly. Em-dashes yes; exclamation marks never.
- **Naming.** Products are named as objects with an article — **The Perfume, The Cream, The Oil, The Langerie, The Elixir** — and a poetic proper name (**Nectar, Gloss, Aura, Lunar Elixir**), sometimes with a parenthetical soul-word: *(Moon) Nectar*, *(Soul) Gloss*, *(Satin) The Langerie*. Perfumes always carry the **index + group**: "004 Citrus — Crispy Orange".
- **Casing.** Body copy sentence case. Structural type — eyebrows, buttons, nav, labels, badges, pack lines — **UPPERCASE, letterspaced**. Brand always **INTIA**.
- **Claims.** Hedged exactly as the label hedges: "helps neutralize", "helps repair the look of damage", "up to 100% stronger than leading commercial benchmarks". Regulatory copy (INCI, PAO, "External use only. Avoid eyes.", the responsible-person block) is reproduced verbatim.
- **Vocabulary that belongs to INTIA:** stillness, presence, whisper, glow, radiance, ritual, tactile, grounded, pearl/nacre, cashmere, silk, satin, longevity, enough. **Avoid:** loud, boost-y or clinical-hype language — "game-changing", "must-have", "hack", "miracle", anything shouted.
- **Emoji: never.** No dingbats. The only non-alphabetic marks are the middot `·`, the multiplication sign `×` (dualities and Material × Pearl), and the em-dash.

Examples to imitate:
> Where radiance whispers.
> Luxury in stillness.
> For women who move in silence but leave presence behind.
> Scent that stays, care that transforms, beauty that whispers.
> Take damage away. Take the city away. Take the stress away.

# VISUAL FOUNDATIONS

**Colour.** A neutral brand with a single metal and a coded accent. The base is **ivory** (`--ivory-100`, warm, never white) and **pearl** (`--pearl-200` = **Pantone 663 C**, the packaging base). Structure comes from **ash** (brushed steel, frosted glass) and **graphite** (`--graphite-900`, the logotype black); warmth from **sand** and **bronze**. The only metal is **rose gold / pink gold mat** (`--rosegold-500`) — always matte, never shiny, used for hairlines, eyebrows on ink, and at most one CTA per screen. Saturated colour enters **only** through the five **perfume group codes** (gourmand, airy, flower, citrus, cherry), and only on the half-circle, the product well and the cap. Never two group colours competing in one composition. Status colours are desaturated and earthy.

**Nacre — the red thread.** Every perfume shares pearl iridescence, so every pearl surface in the UI can carry `--nacre-veil` (35–60%) or `--nacre-sheen`. It must stay a *whisper of inner glow beneath matte restraint*: low opacity, soft-light blend, over a neutral only — never a saturated rainbow, never over a group colour at full strength. On ink, drop it to ~18%.

**Type.** Two families. **Bodoni Moda** (didone, matching the chosen logotype cut) for all display — regular weight, tight leading, italic for pull-quotes. **Jost** (geometric grotesk, echoing the alternate sans logotype studies) at **weight 300** for body, 1.6–1.7 leading. The third voice is a treatment, not a family: **uppercase Jost tracked 0.14–0.30em** for eyebrows, nav, buttons and pack lines. Never bold. Never a third family.

**Shape & radii.** The brief is explicit — *rounded edges, balanced proportions, timeless silhouettes*. Nothing has a hard 90° corner except full-bleed imagery: 6px chips, **10px buttons and fields**, 16px cards, 24px wells, 36px panels, pill for badges and swatches. Two brand geometries: the **dome** (`--radius-dome`, the mark and the group code) and the **capsule** (`--radius-capsule`, the flacon and tube silhouettes).

**Materials & textures.** The named finishes are **cashmere** (soft-touch matte), **fine silk**, **satin**, **lacquer** and **frost glass**, over glass, brushed steel, natural wood and stone-like surfaces. In UI they map to: matte ivory/sand fills, pearl surfaces with `--shadow-pearl` inset, graphite lacquer panels, and `--frost-bg` + `--frost-blur` for the nav and any overlay on imagery.

**Backgrounds.** Flat ivory or pearl first; full-bleed graphite for editorial breaks; full-bleed brand photography for hero and philosophy bands. Gradients only as quiet material washes (`--wash-ivory`, `--wash-pearl`, `--wash-bronze`, `--wash-ink`) — never a decorative "hero gradient", never blue-violet. No patterns, no drawn illustration.

**Imagery.** Warm bronze light raking across graphite shadow; dew, particles and nacre at macro scale; frosted glass diffusing a single soft form; amber glass on stone. Low saturation, one light source, deep charcoal (not black) shadows, visible grain. Cool clinical white-box, high-key beauty retouch and colour-popped stills are off-brand. Real examples live in `assets/imagery/` — they are the client's own brief imagery.

**Cards.** Soft-white surface, 1px `--border-soft` hairline, **16px radius**, `--shadow-soft` at rest. The product well inside a card is a group tint under a nacre veil, with the group dome top-right.

**Shadows.** Two in practice: `--shadow-soft` at rest, `--shadow-lift` on hover (wide, very soft, high offset). `--shadow-pearl` is an inset used to make pearl surfaces read as physical objects. Nothing looks like raised plastic.

**Transparency & blur.** Frost glass for the sticky nav (`--frost-bg` + 18px blur); a flat 42% graphite scrim for modals and the bag drawer; type over photography always sits on `--scrim-bottom`, never on a capsule chip.

**Motion.** One easing, `--ease-quiet` `cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1)`, plus `--ease-breath` for the slow nacre drift; durations 180 / 360 / 720ms. Entrances fade and rise 10px; the drawer slides; the nacre sheen drifts over 9s. Nothing bounces, springs or scales up. `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses everything to 1ms.

**Hover / press / focus.** Solid buttons darken (`graphite-900 → graphite-700`); outline buttons fill with ivory-200; metal buttons fill with rosegold-100; icon buttons take an ivory pill; links keep colour and switch the underline to rose gold; cards lift **-5px** with `--shadow-lift` (the only transform). Press = 0.84 opacity, no shrink. Focus = 1px rose-gold outline, 3px offset. Disabled = 0.36 opacity.

**Layout.** 1280px max content, 40px gutters, 62ch prose, 76px nav, 128px between sections. Two-column splits (image well ↔ text) are the workhorse. The header is the only fixed element.

# ICONOGRAPHY

- **No icon set was supplied.** INTIA UI uses **Lucide** (1.5px stroke) as a documented substitution — its hairline stroke matches the brand's 1px rules. Loaded per glyph from `unpkg.com/lucide-static@0.469.0` and masked so it always inherits `currentColor`. Use the `Icon` component; **never hand-draw SVG**.
- Icons are utility only: nav (`search`, `user`, `shopping-bag`), controls (`plus`, `minus`, `check`, `x`, `chevron-down`, `arrow-right`), and at most one 22px glyph per value block. Never filled, never coloured with a group colour.
- **No emoji, ever.** No unicode dingbats as icons.
- **The dome is the brand's real icon system.** `GroupMark` renders it in a group colour — that is how a fragrance is identified without a packshot. The *logo* mark is never recoloured this way.
